Web Privacy Policy for CricLeague

Effective date: September 30, 2026

Policy type: Web App Privacy Policy

This policy applies to the CricLeague Web App available at cricleague.nrkmart.in.

Privacy-first statement: We do not collect, sell, or share personal data from Web App users.

1. What We Collect

We do not collect personal data through the Web App. We do not run advertising trackers or third-party analytics scripts for user profiling.

2. How Data Is Used in the Web App

  • Match progress, score state, and temporary session details may be stored in your browser for app functionality.
  • This data is used only to provide scoring features and improve your in-app experience.
  • We do not sell or rent any user data.

3. Third-Party Access

We do not share personal data with third-party advertisers or data brokers.

4. Children’s Privacy

The Web App is intended for cricket scoring use and does not knowingly collect personal data from children.

5. Updates to This Policy

We may update this Web Privacy Policy from time to time. Any update will be posted on this page with a revised effective date.

6. Contact

If you have any questions about this Web Privacy Policy, contact us at support@nrkmart.in.
