2. Player identity and team movement

Player identity remains persistent even when the player changes teams.

👤

Player Master

Player is created once with a persistent Player ID. The same identity can be reused throughout the series.

→
🏏

Current Team

The player's current team membership is mutable. Moving the player does not create a new player.

→
📊

Tournament History

Historical matches continue to reference the same Player ID, allowing statistics to be aggregated across team changes.

Core rule:

Player identity and current team membership are separate concepts. A player can move from Team A → Team B → Team A while retaining the same Player ID and cumulative tournament statistics.
