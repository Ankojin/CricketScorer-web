Privacy Policy for CricLeague

Effective date: September 1, 2026

CricLeague (“we,” “our,” or “us”) is built as a privacy-first application. This Privacy Policy explains our policies regarding the collection, use, and disclosure of personal information.

1. Information Collection and Use

CricLeague does not collect, store, transmit, or sell personal data to external servers or third-party advertisers. Match data, team details, player statistics, and scores are saved locally on your device using Room Database.

2. Permissions and Local P2P Sync

CricLeague uses Google Play Services Nearby Connections API to enable local peer-to-peer score sharing between nearby devices.

  • Bluetooth and Wi-Fi Direct permissions (BLUETOOTH_SCAN, BLUETOOTH_CONNECT, and NEARBY_WIFI_DEVICES) are used only to discover and connect to nearby scoring devices.
  • Location permission (ACCESS_FINE_LOCATION) is required by the Android operating system to enable Bluetooth and Wi-Fi Direct scanning. Location data is never recorded, tracked, or transmitted.

3. Children’s Privacy

CricLeague does not address anyone under the age of 13 and does not knowingly collect personally identifiable information from children.

4. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. You are advised to review this page periodically for changes.

5. Contact Us

If you have questions or suggestions regarding this Privacy Policy, contact us at support@nrkmart.in.
