Set up the match — Teams, Settings, Toss

Four-step wizard. Full Match shows the squad builder. Player-count boxes used by Web Score stay hidden.

↓
Step 1 — Teams and squads
  • Name Team A and Team B, and pick each team colour.
  • Load a saved team into either squad, or type a new player and add them.
  • Or open the global player list: search, add one, or add a checked group.
  • On each player: mark Captain, mark Vice-captain, move to the other squad, or remove.
↓ NEXT: OVERS
Did the user add any players?

Empty squads are filled with 11 placeholders (“Team A Player 1” …) so the match can still start. A squad the user built is kept as entered. First player becomes captain and the second vice-captain if those roles were left blank.

↓
Step 2 — Settings
  • Overs per innings: stepper, or 6 / 10 / 20 / 35 / 50.
  • Max overs per bowler. Defaults to about one fifth of the innings, and cannot exceed the innings length.
  • If a series is active, its defaults seed overs, bowler cap, powerplay, quota, and gully rules.
  • Teams and named players are saved for reuse.

Back returns to Teams.

↓ NEXT: TOSS
Step 3 — Coin toss
  • Choose who calls: Team A or Team B.
  • Call Heads or Tails, then flip. Flip again is allowed.
  • The winner chooses Bat first or Bowl first.

Back returns to Settings. Start Scoring Now stays hidden until the coin has landed.

↓ START SCORING NOW
Match is created and goes live
  • Status LIVE, scoring mode FULL, toss winner and decision stored.
  • Batting side and bowling side follow the decision.
  • Opening striker is batting player 1, non-striker is player 2, bowler is bowling player 1.
  • Saved on this device, and synced to the cloud because the user is signed in.

Live hub — what stays on screen for the whole match

Full Match keeps batter and bowler tables, the four view tabs, and the bottom bar. Web Score hides those.

Match views
Summary Scorecard Overs Stats

Bottom bar while scoring: Live, Scorecard, Overs. Leaving the match: Home, Matches, Teams, Series, Stats.

Summary (the scoring screen)
  • Who is batting, score, overs, current run rate, this-over balls.
  • In the chase: target, runs still needed, balls left, required rate.
  • Powerplay label while a powerplay is set.
  • Batter card (striker starred) with Swap and replace. Bowler card with replace.

4

Score a ball

Every legal tap is saved, replayed by the scoring engine, and the screen redraws. A pending choice blocks the next ball until it is answered.

Can this ball be recorded?

No, if a choice is still open, the match is abandoned, or the match is already complete. Abandoned matches resume only from Match settings.

↓ YES — PICK AN OUTCOME
User tapsWhat they confirmWhat happens next
Runs 0, 1, 2, 3, 4, 6 Nothing else. 4 and 6 show as boundary chips. Odd runs rotate the strike. Then the over and innings checks below.
Wide or No-ball How many extra runs. Not a legal delivery, unless gully rules say wides and no-balls add no extra runs.
Byes or Leg byes Runs completed. Counted as a legal ball. Strike rotates on odd totals.
Overthrows A custom run total. Recorded as additional runs on the ball.
Dropped catch Which fielder, then runs completed (0–3). The batter stays in. The drop is stored on that ball.
1G Granted run Nothing else. One granted run. Strike does not rotate.
Wicket Dismissal type, then the details in the next table. A new batter is required before the next ball. Retired hurt replaces the batter without adding a wicket.
Undo Confirm by tapping. Last ball is removed and the innings is recalculated.
Swap Nothing else. Striker and non-striker change ends. Hidden if single-side batting is on.

↓ AVAILABLE ANY TIME THE MATCH IS OPEN
Share live

LIVE builds ?matchId=…, copies it, and opens WhatsApp with the score. A spectator sees the same hub read-only. The link is refused once the match is no longer LIVE.

Match and Gully Rules
  • Change overs, max bowler overs, powerplay, and each side’s wicket-keeper.
  • Gully rules: common player, join mid-match, switch teams, single-side batting, no extra runs for wides and no-balls, last man standing, unequal teams.
  • Abandon or resume. Export or import a backup.
↓
Teams directory

Saved teams and the global player list. Step 1 loads those into a new Full Match squad.

Series

A series can hold default overs and gully rules. A new match copies those defaults while that series is active.

Abandoned

Scoring is locked. Resume from Match settings puts it back to LIVE. Delete from Match Center removes it after a confirm.
